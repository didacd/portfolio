Hi, I'm Didac. Welcome to my personal corner of the internet.

I'm a cloud security engineer based in Barcelona, currently working with Kubernetes, OpenShift, and the infrastructure behind modern telco cloud platforms. This site is where I'll share what I'm learning, the problems I'm exploring, and the occasional project that grows out of that work.

You can expect posts about cloud security, infrastructure, containers, and the tools and ideas I find useful along the way. I also want this to be a place for notes that are worth keeping—and, hopefully, worth sharing.

Thanks for stopping by. If you'd like to follow my work or get in touch, you'll find my GitHub, Mastodon, and email links on the home page.
